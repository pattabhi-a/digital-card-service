Quality Findings

TL;DR

  • 3 controller endpoints swallow exceptions and return HTTP 200 (lines 56-58, 71-73, 92-96 of DigitalCardController).
  • Generic catch (Exception e) appears in PDFCardServiceImpl, EncryptionUtil, DigitalCardServiceImpl, controller — at least 8 occurrences.
  • Magic strings: "AVAILABLE", "NEW", "STORED", "ERROR", "DIGITAL_CARD_SERVICE", "KERNEL", "SIGN" hard-coded in service.
  • Two service classes exceed 300 LOC; mixed responsibilities (orchestration + parsing + persistence).
  • Stack trace logged with DB username concatenated — sensitive context leak (controller:93).
