Business Capability Synthesis

TL;DR
  • 5 business capabilities map to credential lifecycle: Initiation, Verification, Card Generation, Status Tracking, Notification.
  • Capability owner is a single Spring service — high coupling risk.
  • "Revocation" capability is implicit (status update only); no dedicated workflow.
