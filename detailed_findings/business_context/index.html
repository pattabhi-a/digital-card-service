🌍 MOSIP Ecosystem Context

MOSIP (Modular Open-Source Identification Platform) is a government-scale identity platform designed for countries to build digital identity systems. The digital-card-service operates as a critical microservice within this larger ecosystem.

MOSIP Platform Positioning

📋 Digital Card Service Role

The Digital Card Service is responsible for automating the generation of digital identity cards upon successful completion of registration processing. It acts as a post-processing orchestrator that:

  • Triggers digital card creation upon credential issuance
  • Integrates with multiple MOSIP subsystems to collect necessary data
  • Generates secure, tamper-proof PDF cards
  • Encrypts and shares card data securely
  • Manages the lifecycle of digital cards (creation, status tracking, updates)
🔗 Core Responsibility:
When a citizen completes registration and credentials are issued, the Digital Card Service automatically generates a downloadable, cryptographically secure digital identity card (PDF) that serves as portable proof of identity.

🔗 Integration Points

The Digital Card Service coordinates with six major MOSIP subsystems:

1. Registration Processor
Listens for registration completion events. When a registration packet is successfully processed, it triggers credential and digital card generation workflow.
2. Credential Service
Receives credential request events. Digital Card Service monitors credential status and triggers PDF generation when credentials are issued.
3. Key Manager / Crypto Manager
Provides encryption/decryption services for securing card data. All sensitive card content is encrypted before transmission.
4. Data Share Service
Hosts encrypted digital card files. Digital Card Service uploads encrypted PDFs and gets secure download URLs.
5. ID Repository
Fetches citizen identity data (biometrics, demographics) needed to generate cards.
6. Template & PDF Generator Services
Renders card design and generates secure PDF with signatures and watermarks.

👥 User Personas

1. Citizen / End User
Completes registration and receives a downloadable digital identity card. Expects instant generation, easy download, offline accessibility, and secure storage.
2. Registrar / Government Staff
Monitors registration completion and digital card generation status. Needs visibility into card generation failures and ability to retry/resend.
3. Credential Authority
Issues credentials that trigger digital card generation. Needs to track card generation as part of credential lifecycle.
4. System Administrator
Operates and maintains the service. Needs monitoring, logs, configuration management, and disaster recovery capabilities.
5. Architect / Technology Lead
Makes decisions on scalability, security, and technology choices. Needs clear understanding of performance limits and risks.

📚 Key Use Cases

UC-1: Automatic Digital Card Generation
Primary Flow: Credential issued → Event published → Digital Card Service listens → Fetches identity data → Generates PDF → Encrypts → Uploads to DataShare → Updates database
UC-2: Card Status Tracking
Flow: Citizen queries card status API → Service returns status (generated/pending/failed) and download URL if available
UC-3: Card Update on Identity Change
Flow: Citizen updates identity data → Event published → Digital Card Service regenerates card with new data
UC-4: Failed Card Regeneration
Flow: Card generation fails → Service retries with exponential backoff → Alerts administrator if persistent failure
UC-5: Secure Download
Flow: Citizen downloads card via secure link → Gets encrypted PDF → Can decrypt offline with provided key

Identity Lifecycle Phases
